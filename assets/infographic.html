A plain-language app idea in.
A certified, build-ready spec for AI coding agents out.

AI coding agents build exactly what they're told, so a vague or drifting spec becomes a confident, wrong app. This pipeline makes every requirement trace back to something the client actually said, and has an independent reviewer certify it before delivery.

What makes it different

Traceability is the contract

Requirements cite decisions; decisions cite the client's words. Nothing gets invented or silently dropped.

vs. one long prompt that drifts

Generation and review are separated

The reviewer runs in a fresh context from the writer, logs typed findings, and must pass twice before export.

vs. a model grading its own work

Automation with a hard ceiling

The pilot runs stages inside a fixed model-call allowance and stops to explain why when a person has to decide.

vs. agents that loop and spend
